----------------------------------------------------------------------
  OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Wenchuan earthquake was a catastrophic magnitude 7.9 to 8.0 earthquake that struck Wenchuan County, Sichuan Province, China, on May 12, 2008. The disaster resulted in nearly 90,000 deaths or missing individuals, injured hundreds of thousands, and left millions homeless. It caused immense destruction, flattening four-fifths of structures in affected areas and triggering numerous landslides and dam failures.

  SITUATION BRIEF
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Headline : Wenchuan Earthquake Devastates Sichuan, China, Causing Massive Casualties and Damage
  Overview : The Wenchuan earthquake, a massive 8.0 magnitude quake, struck Wenchuan County, Sichuan Province, China, on May 12, 2008, at 14:28 CST. Its epicenter was near Dujiangyan, approximately 80 km west-northwest of Chengdu, causing widespread destruction across southwestern China.
  Severity : The disaster resulted in at least 69,185 people killed, 374,171 injured, and 18,467 missing, with the final official assessment reporting almost 90,000 dead or missing, including over 5,300 children. Over 4.8 million people were left homeless, and economic losses were estimated at 86 billion US dollars.
  Affected : More than 45.5 million people across 10 provinces, including Sichuan, Gansu, and Shaanxi, were affected by the earthquake. Tremors were felt across a vast region of China.
  Response : The Chinese government launched a massive rescue and relief operation, deploying military personnel and medical teams to the affected regions. Rescue efforts were complicated by aftershocks and landslides.
  Key Facts:
    1. The Wenchuan earthquake registered a magnitude of 8.0 Ms.
    2. It occurred on May 12, 2008, at 14:28 China Standard Time.
    3. The disaster led to almost 90,000 people dead or missing, including over 5,300 children.
    4. More than 4.8 million individuals were left homeless across the affected regions.
    5. Economic losses due to the earthquake were estimated at 86 billion US dollars.
